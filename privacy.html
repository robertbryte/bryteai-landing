Privacy Policy

How Bryte AI collects, uses, and protects information processed through our platform.

📅 Effective Date: September 25, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 25, 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how Bryte AI (operated by Bryte Tech Solutions, "we," "us," or "our") processes information in connection with our custom AI support agent platform. If you are interacting with a support channel powered by Bryte AI, this policy applies to you.

1. Who We Are

Bryte AI is an AI-powered support automation platform that processes incoming support tickets on behalf of its clients. In this context, Bryte AI acts as a data processor for each client organization, which serves as the data controller responsible for their users' and visitors' personal information.

For questions about how a specific organization collects and uses your data more broadly, please refer to that organization's own privacy policy.

2. Information We Process

When you submit a support request through a channel powered by Bryte AI, the following categories of information may be processed:

  • Support ticket content — the text of your request, questions, or issue description
  • Contact information — name and email address as provided in the support ticket
  • Account or membership identifiers — if included in the ticket by the support platform
  • Metadata — timestamps, ticket IDs, and channel source (e.g., Help Scout, Zendesk)

We do not collect payment information, passwords, or sensitive personal data as part of ticket processing.

3. How We Use This Information

Information processed by Bryte AI is used solely to:

  • Generate a draft response to your support inquiry using the client's knowledge base
  • Route and categorize tickets according to the client's configured workflow rules
  • Provide performance reporting to the client organization (aggregate metrics only — no individual PII in reports)
  • Improve the quality and accuracy of AI-drafted responses for the client's support team

We do not use your information to train general-purpose AI models, advertise to you, or sell your data to third parties.

4. PII Scrubbing

Before any ticket content is sent to an AI language model for processing, Bryte AI automatically detects and removes Personally Identifiable Information (PII) — including names, email addresses, phone numbers, and account numbers. This scrubbing step is applied consistently to every ticket processed through our system.

The scrubbed (de-identified) content is what the AI model uses to generate a draft reply. Original ticket data containing PII remains within the secure support platform (e.g., Help Scout) operated by the client organization.

5. Human Review Before Any Response Is Sent

No AI-generated response is ever sent directly to you. Every draft produced by Bryte AI is posted as an internal note visible only to the client's support team. A human agent reviews, edits if needed, and approves the reply before it reaches you. You always interact with a human-reviewed response.

6. Data Retention

Bryte AI does not independently store your personal information long-term. Ticket data remains within the client's support platform under their data retention policies. Any temporary processing logs Bryte AI maintains are retained for no more than 30 days and are then permanently deleted.

7. Data Sharing and Sub-Processors

To operate the Bryte AI service, we use the following categories of sub-processors:

  • AI language model providers — receive de-identified (PII-scrubbed) ticket content only
  • Cloud infrastructure providers — hosting and compute for the Bryte AI platform
  • Support platform APIs — to read incoming tickets and post draft responses (e.g., Help Scout, Zendesk)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for their own purposes.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security controls, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (TLS 1.2+) for all API communications
  • Access controls limiting who can view ticket data within the Bryte AI platform
  • Automated PII scrubbing prior to AI model calls
  • Regular security reviews of our infrastructure and dependencies

9. Your Rights

Because Bryte AI processes your data on behalf of client organizations, requests related to your personal data — including access, correction, deletion, or portability — should be directed to the organization whose support channel you contacted. They are the data controller and are responsible for honoring these requests.

If you believe Bryte AI has processed your data in violation of this policy, you may also contact us at privacy@brytetech.solutions.

10. Cookies and Tracking

The Bryte AI support agent itself does not set cookies or use tracking technologies. Any cookies or analytics on a client's website are governed by that organization's own privacy policy.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted a support ticket containing personal data, please contact the relevant organization's support team to have it removed.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated to our client organizations. Continued use of a Bryte AI-powered support channel after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

For privacy-related questions about the Bryte AI platform:
